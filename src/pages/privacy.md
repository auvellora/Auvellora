This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this website, contact us or buy from us, why we use it, and what rights you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to answer you and to make and deliver your piece.

## Who we are

The data controller is the business named at the top of this page. You can reach us about anything in this policy at [auvellora@gmail.com](mailto:auvellora@gmail.com).

## What we collect and why

**When you browse the website**

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the website sets no cookies. Like every website, it is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers briefly record technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We use this only for that purpose (our legitimate interest in running a safe website, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

**When you contact us**

If you write to us by email or through the contact form, we receive your name, email address, what you're interested in, your message, and anything you choose to send, such as photos and dimensions of your space. We use this to answer you and, if you ask, to prepare an offer (steps before a contract, Article 6(1)(b), or our legitimate interest in answering general questions, Article 6(1)(f)).

**When you buy or commission a piece**

To agree, make, deliver and, where relevant, install your piece, we also need your phone number, delivery or installation address, billing details and the details of your order (Article 6(1)(b)). We keep invoices and accounting records because the law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).

You don't have to give us your data, but without it we can't answer you or fulfil an order. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for automated decisions about you, and we don't send you marketing without asking first.

## Who else sees your data

We share data only with services that help us run the business, and only as far as they need it:

- our email provider (Google), which hosts our mailbox;
- our website hosting provider;
- a form-delivery service, if we use one to deliver contact-form messages to our mailbox;
- couriers or transport companies delivering your piece;
- our accountant and, when the law requires it, public authorities.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area (EEA), for example in the United States. When data leaves the EEA, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries that don't lead to an order: up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted.
- Orders and commissions: as long as needed to complete the order and handle any guarantee claim.
- Invoices and accounting records: as long as Latvian accounting law requires.
- Technical records kept by the hosting provider: a short time, usually days or weeks.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct it if it's wrong;
- delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
- restrict or object to how we use it;
- send it to you or to another company in a common format.

Write to [auvellora@gmail.com](mailto:auvellora@gmail.com) and we'll answer within one month. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (Datu valsts inspekcija, [www.dvi.gov.lv](https://www.dvi.gov.lv)) or to the data protection authority where you live.

## Cookies

The website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Its fonts are served from our own website, so your browser doesn't contact other companies when you visit. If we ever add tools that use cookies, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
