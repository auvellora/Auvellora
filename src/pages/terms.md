These terms apply when you buy an Element, or commission a piece or an interior, directly from Auvellora, for example by email or phone after contacting us through this website. If you buy one of our pieces through Etsy, Etsy's terms and our Etsy shop policies apply to that order instead.

## 1. Who we are

The seller is the business named at the top of this page. You can contact us at [auvellora@gmail.com](mailto:auvellora@gmail.com).

## 2. Every piece is one of a kind

Every piece is made by hand from found and reclaimed wood. Cracks, knots, bark marks, changes in colour and small movements in the wood are natural features of the material, not defects. The photos on this website show examples: the piece you receive will be similar, but never identical.

## 3. How an order is made

1. You tell us what you're interested in.
2. We send you a written offer by email: the piece or the work, the price, delivery or installation, the estimated timing and the payment terms.
3. The contract is made when you accept the offer in writing and we confirm it.

Prices are on request and are given in our offer, including any taxes that apply. Delivery, installation and any other costs are shown separately in the offer.

## 4. Payment

You pay by bank transfer to the account on our invoice, as set out in the offer. For commissions, and for pieces made or adapted for you, we usually ask for an advance payment before we start. The piece remains ours until it has been paid for in full.

## 5. Delivery and collection

We deliver within Latvia, and elsewhere by agreement. The estimated delivery date and the delivery cost are in our offer. You can also collect your piece by arrangement. Please check your piece when it arrives and tell us about any transport damage as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours. Responsibility for the piece passes to you when it is delivered to you.

## 6. Your right to change your mind (consumers)

If you are a consumer and you bought a ready-made Element at a distance, for example by email, you can withdraw from the contract within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

- To withdraw, tell us clearly before the 14 days are over, by email to [auvellora@gmail.com](mailto:auvellora@gmail.com). You can use the withdrawal form at the end of this page, but you don't have to.
- Send or bring the piece back within 14 days of telling us. Our pieces are large and usually need a courier: you pay the direct cost of returning it, and we tell you the estimated cost in our offer.
- We refund everything you paid, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal, using the same payment method. We may wait until we have the piece back.
- Please handle the piece only as you would in a shop. If its value goes down because it was handled more than needed to check it, we may deduct that from the refund.

**When the right to withdraw doesn't apply**

- Pieces made to your specifications or clearly personalised for you, such as commissions and Elements adapted for you (height, branch, light).
- Installation and other services that we have fully carried out with your prior agreement, after you confirmed that you understand you lose the right to withdraw once the work is done.

## 7. If something is wrong

Your piece must match what we agreed. If it doesn't, for example an electrical part fails, or the piece is damaged in a way that isn't a natural feature of the wood, please tell us. As a consumer, you have the legal right to make a claim within 2 years of delivery, and we will repair or replace the piece, lower the price or refund you, as the law provides.

This doesn't cover the natural features of wood described in section 2, normal wear, or damage caused by misuse, by use outdoors or in damp rooms, or by not following our care advice.

## 8. Lamps and safety

Our lamps are made for indoor use in dry rooms. Pendant lamps that hang from the ceiling must be connected to the mains by a qualified electrician. Please use the bulbs or light sources we recommend for each piece.

## 9. Commissions and interiors

For commissions, we agree the design, scale, materials, price and timeline with you in writing before we start. Because every branch is unique, the finished piece may differ slightly from sketches or visualisations. Changes after work has started may change the price and timing; we agree them with you first. If installation is part of the order, please make sure we can reach the space and that it is ready on the agreed day.

## 10. Questions and complaints

Please contact us first: we'll do our best to put things right, and we reply to complaints within 15 days. If we can't resolve it together, consumers in Latvia can turn to the Consumer Rights Protection Centre (Patērētāju tiesību aizsardzības centrs, [www.ptac.gov.lv](https://www.ptac.gov.lv)), including its Consumer Disputes Resolution Commission. Consumers in other EU countries can get free help from the European Consumer Centre in their country.

Latvian law applies to these terms. If you are a consumer living in another EU country, you also keep the protection of the mandatory consumer laws of the country where you live.
